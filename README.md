# 📱 Google Play Store Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Google Play Store dataset to understand application characteristics, user engagement, pricing strategies, and factors influencing app popularity.

The analysis includes data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering, visualization, and business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze application ratings and user engagement.
- Compare free and paid applications.
- Identify high-performing categories.
- Understand pricing and monetization strategies.
- Generate actionable business insights for developers.

---

## 📂 Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle – Google Play Store Apps Dataset
- **Platform:** https://www.kaggle.com/

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Most applications have ratings above **4.0**.
- Free applications dominate the Play Store and receive significantly higher install counts.
- App ratings alone have little influence on popularity.
- App size has minimal impact on installs.
- Video Players & Editors category achieves the highest average installs per app.
- Education is highly competitive but does not rank among the top categories by average installs.
- Categories like Comics, Music, and Parenting present lower competition and potential growth opportunities.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Improve app discoverability through App Store Optimization (ASO).
- Adopt a freemium monetization strategy for mass-market applications.
- Focus on quality and differentiation in competitive categories.
- Explore underserved categories with lower competition.
- Invest in premium apps for Health & Fitness and Lifestyle segments.

---

## 📈 Project Outcome

This analysis demonstrates how exploratory data analysis can uncover meaningful business insights that support better product strategy, monetization decisions, and market positioning.
